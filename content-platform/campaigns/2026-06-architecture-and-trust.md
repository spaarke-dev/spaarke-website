# Architecture and Trust

## Theme

The launch month establishes what Spaarke is. June establishes how
it runs. The argument is structural: legal-ops platforms that sit
in their vendor's cloud carry an architectural cost the customer
absorbs forever. Spaarke runs in the customer's own M365 tenant,
governed by their identity, DLP, and retention policies. The June
arc is for the IT decision-maker, the GC who has to convince the
CIO, and the legal-ops leader who has to defend the deployment
choice to security review.

## Why now

Two pressures converge in June. Mid-year tech-stack reviews start
in many enterprise legal departments. And the AI-procurement
conversation — driven by 2026's governance scrutiny — increasingly
hinges on where data sits at training and inference time. The
Microsoft-tenant story is the right one for both audiences at once.

## Narrative arc

1. **Why we built on Microsoft** — the strategic case for the
   M365 stack as the operating layer for legal
2. **Why human-in-the-loop is a competitive advantage** *(new
   Jun 2)* — the philosophical pairing: trust isn't just where
   the data lives, it's who makes the call
3. **Tenant-Dedicated Deployment** — the deployment model that
   makes "in your tenant" a structural fact, not a marketing line
4. **Your legal data belongs to you** — the data-sovereignty
   case for engagement-boundary AI grounding
5. **Spaarke for your IT team** — what IT signs off on
6. **Why Microsoft is becoming the operating layer for legal**
   *(new Jun 23)* — the macro thesis the rest of the campaign
   rests on
7. **Document management is becoming document intelligence**
   *(new Jun 30, practitioner byline)* — the SharePoint Embedded
   case for matter-aware document infrastructure
