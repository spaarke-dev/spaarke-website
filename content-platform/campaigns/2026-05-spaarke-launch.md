# Spaarke Launch

## Theme

Spaarke comes out of stealth as a Legal Operations Intelligence
platform — Microsoft-native, AI-directed, human-controlled, deployed
inside the customer's own tenant. The launch month establishes both
the platform and the category. Welcome to Spaarke is the front door.
The two foundational pieces (What is Legal Operations Intelligence,
The Legal IQ Stack) carry the structural argument behind it. Two
new May calendar pieces extend the operating-model thesis into the
near term.

## Why now

The first wave of legal-AI productivity tools has saturated.
Generative-AI use among in-house departments roughly doubled
2024→2025 but hasn't translated into operating-model change.
There's an opening for the operating-model argument and a small
window before the field crowds with similar framing.

## Narrative arc

A reader who follows this campaign in order should land on:

1. **Welcome to Spaarke** — what we are and the philosophy
   (AI-directed, human-controlled, in the customer's tenant)
2. **What is Legal Operations Intelligence?** — the category
   argument that the platform sits inside
3. **The Legal IQ Stack: Data, Memory, Inference** — the three-
   layer architecture that turns LOI into a working system
4. **Legal IQ and the future of legal operations** *(new May 19)* —
   the forward-looking case that LOI is the next layer above the
   ELM stack
5. **AI is moving from tools to operating models** *(new May 26)* —
   the macro framing that the rest of the year unpacks

LinkedIn distribution mirrors the same arc — each week is one step
in the argument.
