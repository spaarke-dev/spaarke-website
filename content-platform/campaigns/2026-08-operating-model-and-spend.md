# Operating Model and Spend

## Theme

The fall arc is for the legal-ops practitioner. Where May/June/July
made the case for what Spaarke is, how it runs, and how AI works
in it, August/September make the case for what an LOI operating
model actually does — for spend, for memory, for cross-functional
visibility, and for the maturity arc the department is on.

This is a longer window (8 weeks) on purpose. Most of the existing
library lands here — the operating-model arguments are dense and
benefit from being spaced out across the back-to-school window
when budget conversations and Q4 planning are starting.

## Why now

Q4 budgeting starts in late August / early September in most
enterprises. Legal-ops practitioners are building the case for
2027 investment, defending headcount, and answering CFO questions
about spend. The campaign meets that conversation.

## Narrative arc

1. **The $20B blind spot** — the structural challenge with legal
   spend visibility (existing, recently revised)
2. **Breaking the silo between legal, finance, and the business** —
   why cross-functional visibility is architectural, not
   aspirational (existing, recently revised)
3. **Institutional knowledge** — the memory case: what walks out
   the door when senior staff leave (existing)
4. **From reactive to predictive: the LOI maturity model** —
   the diagnostic frame for where a department sits today
   (existing)
5. **Legal ops is not IT for lawyers** — the role-and-mandate
   piece (existing)
6. **Spaarke feature specification** — the comprehensive
   reference; appropriate near the end of the campaign for
   prospects ready to evaluate
7. **The rise of AI-directed legal workflows** *(new white paper,
   target Q3)* — the macro architecture argument tying the
   campaign together
8. **e-Invoicing and ViDA: what corporate legal needs to know**
   *(promote from Q3 idea bucket)* — the regulatory wedge that
   makes spend infrastructure non-optional
