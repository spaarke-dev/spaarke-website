# Spaarke Legal AI Accelerator

## Theme

Microsoft now offers an enterprise AI platform on which legal teams can build
the legal AI they need, inside the environment they already govern. The
Spaarke Legal AI Accelerator for Microsoft Copilot supplies the core legal
skills, the practice packs, the tools and connectors, and the forward-deployed
legal engineers to put it to work in days. Everything it builds carries
forward onto the Legal Operations Intelligence platform. Every asset leads to
one action: a meeting with Spaarke.

## Why now

Every legal team is under pressure to settle its AI strategy and show results.
Microsoft's platform makes it possible to build legal AI inside the
organization's own environment, and the campaign launches ahead of Microsoft
Ignite (17 to 20 November 2026).
