# Spaarke Legal AI Accelerator

## Theme

Microsoft now offers an enterprise AI platform on which legal teams can build
the legal AI they need, inside the environment they already govern. The
Spaarke Legal AI Accelerator for Microsoft Copilot supplies the core legal
skills, the practice packs, the tools and connectors, and the forward-deployed
legal engineers to put it to work in days. Everything it builds carries
forward onto the Legal Operations Intelligence platform. Every asset leads to
one action: a meeting with Spaarke.

## Why now

Every legal team is under pressure to settle its AI strategy and show results.
Microsoft's platform makes it possible to build legal AI inside the
organization's own environment, and the campaign launches ahead of Microsoft
Ignite (17 to 20 November 2026).

### Approach

The accelerator is a concrete offer with a single next step, a 30-minute
scoping session, so this campaign is built to convert, not only to reach. It
follows the procedure's default pattern: warm a cold audience with the
argument, give it something of value in exchange for contact, then ask the
people who engaged for the meeting.

1. **Warm (route C and A).** The founder post as a Thought Leader Ad, and the
   two exhibits as single image ads to the article. These build the
   engagement audience (W2) that the later stages need.
2. **Convert with value (route B).** Gated Document ads: the accelerator brief
   for legal operations and law firm audiences, and a readiness checklist for
   IT and general counsel, both with a Lead Gen Form.
3. **Ask for the meeting (route E).** People who engaged (W2) get the direct
   offer: a single image ad with a Lead Gen Form that requests a scoping
   session. A Conversation ad from the founder is the second test on the same
   audience.

The owner set the budget on 2026-11-03 (amount and split in the private
archive) and asked for an immediate start. At that budget the warm and convert
stages run together from 2026-11-04 rather than in sequence, so the Document
ads are in market before Microsoft Ignite (17 to 20 November). The money goes
to the founder post and the two documents; the exhibit ads and the
Conversation ad are not funded.

Each form carries a 1104 by 400 banner (LinkedIn asks for 552 by 200): the document's headline in teal and coral on a light background, centered because the form preview crops to the middle band. M&A and patents and trademarks were cut from the options to keep the list to six; those practices answer "not sure yet" and are caught in the scoping session. The practice question is the qualification signal, and it tells the legal
engineer what to prepare for the session.

### Copy rules for this campaign

- No competitor names in any ad, form, or document (procedure 7.6). The
  article and the company post name Harvey and Legora; the ads do not.
- Ads carry only claims the article supports. The writer's assertions about
  Copilot replacing other tools, approval without a new security review, and
  usage-based billing stay out of paid copy (procedure 7.6: do not pay to
  promote a claim that has not cleared source discipline).
- Describe Microsoft products by name and do not imply that Microsoft
  endorses or partners with Spaarke. No Microsoft logos in ad creative.
- No prices, packages, or availability dates.
- Run `npm run voice:lint` on this file and on the documents before build.

Accelerator brief, page by page:

1. Cover: The Spaarke Legal AI Accelerator for Microsoft Copilot. One practice, one week.

3. How legal AI on Microsoft is built (Exhibit 1).

Readiness checklist, page by page:

1. The questions legal and IT settle together (the preview page).
2. For IT: licenses, the Cowork usage policy, connector and plugin approval in the registry, sensitivity labels, retention in Purview, and the deployment route (self-deployed or IT-deployed, including Information Barriers).
3. For legal: the first practice and two or three tasks, today's baseline, the team's positions and fallbacks, the AI work already under way, and who owns each position.
