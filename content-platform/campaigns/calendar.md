## Pacing principle

| Cadence | Use |
|---|---|
| ~3 LI/week | Launch sprint only (e.g., May 11–31). Not sustainable. |
| ~2 LI/week | Sustainable working pace |
| ~1 LI/week | Conservative — leaves room for new ideas to land between planned pieces |

The 8-week August/September window is intentionally lower-cadence to leave breathing room.

## How to update this file

When campaign status changes (planned → scheduled → active → complete), edit the **Status** column. When a campaign is created or retired, edit the row. Keep `Q4 (TBD)` row at the bottom as a forward-looking placeholder until it gets named.
