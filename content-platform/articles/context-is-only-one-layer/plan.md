### Beat 1 — Acknowledge the premise (and the market moment)

- **Claim:** The field is converging on the idea that context — what a system knows about a matter, a counterparty, a history — is the prize. Many of this week's announcements get that part right.

### Beat 2 — Name the gap

- **Claim:** A graph of documents and people is one layer. The Legal IQ stack runs in three: **Data** (the unified record), **Memory** (operational history and connections — what a context graph addresses), and **Inference** (AI directed at the grounded record).
- **Move:** Introduce the IQ stack structurally — not name-checked, used. Bold the three layers in the post body (Unicode-safe alternative: no formatting; let sentence structure carry it).
- **Length:** ~2 sentences.
- **Cross-link target:** [The Legal IQ Stack: Data, Memory, Inference](/why-spaarke/the-iq-stack)

### Beat 3 — Why the layer matters

- **Claim:** A context graph built on a DMS sees documents, people, and the relationships between them. An operating model sees matters, projects, invoices, spend, counsel performance, OCG compliance, and outcomes — on the same record, with AI directed at all of it.
- **Move:** Concrete contrast. Operator-grade — list the operational dimensions an operating model covers that a DMS-graph does not.
- **Length:** ~2 sentences.

### Beat 4 — The structural difference

- **Claim:** And when the platform runs inside the customer's own tenant, the engagement boundary doesn't fragment AI grounding. The firm and the in-house team work against the same matter record, the same memory.
- **Move:** Subtle architectural point — without naming "tenant-adjacent" or competitors. The reader who's read Spaarke before will recognize it; the reader who hasn't gets the gist.
- **Length:** ~2 sentences.
- **Cross-link target:** [Institutional Knowledge Is Walking Out the Door](/why-spaarke/institutional-knowledge) (the long form of why this matters)

### Beat 5 — Bring it home

- **Claim:** This is what an operating model looks like, in practice. A graph is necessary; it is not the destination.
- **Move:** Short, clean. One sentence ideally.
- **Length:** ~1 sentence.
