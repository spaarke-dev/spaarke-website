## Post text

Generative AI makes attorney work product more efficient. The business of law requires something different: a system that understands the business, brings together information from across systems, and surfaces the decisions required to address legal issues wherever they arise.

Legal runs on decisions. Spaarke makes them intelligent. The nine slides show how it works: an intelligence layer across your systems of record, a worked example in invoice review, and a platform that runs inside Outlook, Word, and Teams, where your team already works.

We start with one decision. Forward-deployed legal engineers deliver a quick start on your own data, and the model grows one decision at a time through the five phases of the Spaarke Method. Our article sets out how an engagement runs:

https://www.spaarke.com/why-spaarke/the-spaarke-method

Which decision would your team start with? We are forming an advisory group of legal operations leaders in law departments and law firms. Comment or message us to join.

### Slide 1: cover

- Eyebrow: Spaarke overview
- Headline: Legal runs on decisions. Spaarke makes them intelligent.
- Body: Spaarke brings your data, rules, and people into one model, so AI proposes with the full picture and your team makes the call.
- Card: Matter 2041 is at 92% of budget, and this firm's last three invoices needed the same change.

### Slide 2: problem

- Eyebrow: The problem
- Headline: Your legal team makes thousands of decisions. What does it learn from them?
- Body: The evidence sits in matters, invoices, contracts, documents, and email, in systems never designed to be read together. The reasoning behind each decision is rarely kept, so the next one starts from scratch.

### Slide 3: ontology

- Eyebrow: The intelligence layer
- Headline: Spaarke joins them in one living model
- Body: Built on top of your systems of record, the ontology records what exists, how each thing connects, and what can be done. A matter carries its counsel, budget, invoices, guidelines, and decisions together.

### Slide 4: architecture

- Eyebrow: One architecture
- Headline: One intelligence layer across your systems of record
- Body: Spaarke brings matters, documents, and spend together in its own system of record and works alongside the systems you keep. The ontology above them is the layer that AI, reporting, and automation draw on.

### Slide 5: invoice

- Eyebrow: Use case: invoice review
- Headline: From billing guideline to recorded decision
- Body: Guidelines become rules that cite their clause. Every invoice line is checked against the rules in force, and each decision is recorded with its rule and evidence.

### Slide 6: ai

- Eyebrow: Spaarke AI
- Headline: AI-directed, human-controlled
- Body: AI drafts rules, reads each invoice, and suggests the adjustment. A person confirms it, and each decision becomes knowledge for the next.

### Slide 7: work

- Eyebrow: Where you work
- Headline: Built for where your team already works
- Body: Spaarke runs inside Copilot, Outlook, Word, Teams, and SharePoint, so your team keeps the tools it already knows.

### Slide 8: method

- Eyebrow: The Spaarke Method
- Headline: Five phases, and the model grows each cycle
- Body: Each decision moves through five phases, led by forward-deployed legal engineers working with your practitioners and end users. Start with one high-value decision, measure the result, and expand from there.

### Slide 9: close

- Eyebrow: Spaarke at a glance
- Headline: Start with one decision. Build intelligence with every decision after.
- Body: Technology, consulting, and managed services work together as one integrated platform and service. Forward-deployed legal engineers work inside your team, deliver a quick start, and run and grow the platform with you.

Slide 7 shows Spaarke inside Copilot, Outlook, Word and Excel, Teams, and SharePoint, deployed in the customer's tenant or hosted by Spaarke.
