## Spine

> A legal department decides the same things every week, and the evidence for
> each decision sits in separate systems while the reasoning is rarely kept.
> Spaarke builds one model of those decisions, the ontology, on Microsoft,
> and the department owns it. Technology,
> consulting, and managed services are three ways into that one model, and
> invoice review shows the model at work: a guideline becomes a cited rule, the
> rule checks each line, and a person confirms the decision on the record. The
> Spaarke Method builds the model one decision at a time.

**Refrain** (cover, slide 3, close): "one model of your department's
decisions". The wording is fixed here and held at all three anchors.

### Slide 4: the ontology (light)
- **Eyebrow:** "The ontology"
- **Headline:** "A living model of your legal department"
- **Body:** "It records what exists, how each thing connects, and what can be

### Slide 5: one architecture (dark)
- **Eyebrow:** "One architecture"

  uses at the bottom (Decisions, Reporting, Automation).

### Slide 6: invoice review (light)
- **Eyebrow:** "Use case: invoice review"
- **Headline:** "From billing guideline to recorded decision"
- **Body:** "Guidelines become rules that cite their clause. Invoices arrive by
  portal upload, as PDF or LEDES, or from your e-billing system. Every line
  is checked, and each decision is recorded with its rule and evidence."
- **Visual:** four steps top to bottom (Guidelines to rules, Invoices in,
  Rule check, Decision), with one example card: "Line 14 bills $950 an hour,
  and the rate card allows $850 (section 3.2). Suggested: reduce to $850."
- **Note:** the busiest slide. If the body runs past 40 words at draft, the
  intake sentence moves into the visual's labels.

### Slide 7: AI and people (dark)
- **Eyebrow:** "Spaarke AI"
- **Headline:** "AI-directed, human-controlled"
- **Body:** "AI drafts rules from your guidelines, reads each invoice, and
  suggests the adjustment. A person confirms it, and the record shows who
  decided and why."
- **Visual:** two columns, "AI proposes" and "A person decides", joined to a
  record card (decision, rule, evidence, approver).

### Slide 8: your tenant (light)
- **Eyebrow:** "Deployment"
- **Headline:** "Built on Microsoft, in your tenant or hosted by Spaarke"
- **Body:** "Spaarke runs on Microsoft 365, Power Platform, and Dataverse.
  Deploy it in your own Microsoft tenant, under your identity, access
  control, and security policies, or have Spaarke host it for you."
- **Visual:** two options side by side, "In your tenant" and "Hosted by
  Spaarke", on a shared Microsoft foundation.

### Slide 9: the Spaarke Method (dark)
- **Eyebrow:** "The Spaarke Method"
- **Headline:** "Built one decision at a time"
- **Body:** "Forward-deployed legal engineers, practitioners who work inside
  your team, take each decision through five phases. Each cycle ends with a
  measured result and the next decision."
- **Visual:** the five phases (Envision, Design, Build, Deploy, Manage) as a
  numbered vertical loop with a return arrow.

### Slide 10: close (blue or dark)
