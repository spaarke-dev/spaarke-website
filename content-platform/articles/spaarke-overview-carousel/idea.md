# Spaarke overview carousel for LinkedIn

Captured from the writer's session on 2026-10-21. The requests, as stated:

- "let's create a LinkedIn Overview Presentation deck that fits LinkedIn standards"
- "keep this - a new" (the existing full and short Slides decks stay as they are; this is a new deck)
- "and you can consolidate, make new slides, etc. And re: 'capabilties that haven't shipped' we are working on these areas now so it is fine to include them"

Context from the same session:

- Source material is the "Spaarke Overview: Short Version" Slides deck
  (https://claude.ai/artifact/MBcFpA48TBGjnGjJqVjKmE) and the full deck
  (https://claude.ai/artifact/6hiSUnBJYrMZaeP9MzAcJS): Spaarke at a glance
  (technology, consulting, and managed services joined by one ontology the
  customer owns), built natively on Microsoft, five modules on one data model,
  the ontology and one matter in it, one architecture (sources to ontology to
  decisions, BI, and automation), invoice review as a use case, Spaarke AI,
  deployment in the customer's own tenant, managed services, partners, and the
  five-phase Spaarke Method with forward-deployed legal engineers.
- The decks were built for presenting on a 16:9 screen. Posting them to
  LinkedIn as they stand fails on a phone: the diagram images carry small text,
  and 14 slides is more than a carousel holds.
- LinkedIn calibration (`content-types/linkedin-post.md`): 5 to 10 slides, one
  idea per slide, the cover is the hook and the thumbnail, the last slide
  carries the close, 1 to 3 sentences per body slide, mobile first.
- Format decisions from the session: portrait 4:5 (1080 by 1350), uploaded as a
  PDF document post; large type; no website diagram screenshots; the URL goes in
  the post text, because links inside a LinkedIn document are not clickable.
- Standing rules: never name the firm whose operating model the services follow; one architecture, never "two deployment
  modes"; Spaarke is not an e-billing platform, so invoice review is a use case
  of the ontology.
