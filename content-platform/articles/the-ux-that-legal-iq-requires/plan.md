## Spine

Users now expect to interact with enterprise systems differently —
across AI outputs *and* deterministic ones like search, reports,
and workflows. Conversational AI was the right primary UX for the
first wave of generative AI tasks (attorney drafting, document
Q&A). But adding a conversational surface to an existing
application does not embed AI into the system and does not meet
those new UX expectations. Legal IQ — AI inference embedded in a
system of record alongside deterministic facts — is where this
matters most for legal practice, and meeting it requires a
different UX: active engagement with information, organized
around three coordinated surfaces (Assistant, Workspace, Context)
plus inline AI inside content.

### Section 3 — Adding a conversational pane doesn't embed AI

- **Claim:** A conversational surface bolted onto a forms-and-
  clicks application leaves the underlying interaction structure
  unchanged. The user still fills forms, still pages through

  Calling that "AI-native" is the marketing-led error.

### Section 4 — What active engagement with information requires

- **Claim:** The UX shift is broader than AI. Users now expect to
  interact with reports, search results, workflows, matter lists,
  and dashboards — not just read them. Six interaction patterns
  follow, and each applies across AI-derived AND deterministic
  outputs (except pattern 4, which is probabilistic-specific).
- **Evidence:** The six patterns from the brief, each named and
  illustrated with both a deterministic and a probabilistic
  example. Sketched here:
  1. **Active interaction over passive viewing** — pivot, drill,
     follow-up, change the lens without leaving the surface.
     Det: matter list refined by NL ("show only matters with
     budget overruns this quarter"); report drilled to driving
     firms. Prob: AI-summarized matter pivot-able to underlying
     documents.
  2. **Multi-modal intent capture** — form, structured query,
     natural-language, selection; affordances picked per task,
     not forced into one surface.
  3. **Grounding and provenance for every system output** —
     pivot from insight to what produced it. Det: billing-report
     aggregation drillable to invoices; workflow decision
     traceable to the OCG rule. Prob: AI classification linkable
     to grounding documents.
  4. **Inference vs facts, visibly distinguished** (probabilistic-
     specific) — consistent visual treatment + confidence cues.
  5. **Action from insight, in place** — actions one step from
     the insight surface. Det: workflow exception resolved
     inline. Prob: AI-flagged OCG concern accepted/rejected/
     escalated without leaving the invoice.
  6. **Cross-surface continuity** — actions and context carry
     across surfaces; pinned items, refined searches recognized.
- **Cross-link:** [Legal AI Is Not Deterministic — And That
  Matters](`/blog/probabilistic-vs-deterministic`) — cite at
  pattern 4 (the probabilistic-specific one), as the bedrock for
  why inference must be visibly distinguished.
- **Voice notes:** The structural payload of the piece. Each
  pattern lands as a bolded lead-in phrase (per style-guide §4
  on bolded lead-ins) followed by plain explanation + paired
  examples. Use `the-iq-stack`'s side-by-side voice pattern as
  the model — show the difference, don't describe it. Refer to

### Section 5 — The Spaarke shape: three surfaces

- **Claim:** Active engagement is what Spaarke organizes around —
  three coordinated surfaces (Assistant, Workspace, Context) plus
  inline AI inside content and continuity across surfaces. Not
  one surface that does everything; not three siloed ones either.
- **Evidence:** Each surface described conceptually + grounded
  with real Spaarke capabilities. Concreteness level (b) per the
  brief: conceptual + named product capabilities, no
  screenshots.
  - **Assistant** — matter Q&A, find-similar, summarization,
    drafting, the Copilot-native experiences in Microsoft 365.
  - **Workspace** — matter records with AI summaries, intake with
    similar-matter suggestions, document records with
    relationship graphs, invoice review with OCG flags, daily
    briefings, Power BI dashboards on the Dataverse model.
  - **Context** — provenance, evidence, audit; describe
    conceptually rather than as one branded feature.
  - **Inline AI inside content** — Word in-place co-authoring
    with grounded Copilot; selection-driven AI actions inside
    documents and analysis surfaces.
  - **Cross-surface integration** — shared session and context;
    user-visible behavior (asking the Assistant about a matter
    pins it in the Workspace; selecting text deepens the
    Assistant's context).
- **Cross-link:** [The Legal IQ Stack: Data, Memory, Inference]

  "what we mean by AI embedded in a system of record." Light

  category-level prior.
- **Voice notes:** This section is the "show, don't tell." Land
  each surface in two or three sentences; don't over-elaborate.
  The Cross-surface integration sub-section is what ties the
