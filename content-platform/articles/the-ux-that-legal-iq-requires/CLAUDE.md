## 6. What you don't do here

- Do not auto-publish. Final publish is human-driven.
- Do not fabricate stats, sources, or quotes. Mark unverified
  claims `**TBD — confirm**`.
- Do not skip the outline gate. Do not draft before `plan.md` is
  approved.
- Do not invent new tag values. Tags must come from the canonical
  taxonomy in `../../voice/taxonomy.md`.
- **Do not use "chat bubble" or "chat bot" phrasing.** Use
  *conversational surface*, *conversational AI*, *the Assistant
  surface*, *first-generation generative AI*. Plain "chat" is OK
  as a verb (what the user does), not as a label for the UX.
- **Do not cite external research in the published prose.** Menlo
  VC, ServiceNow, Nielsen Norman Group, Don Norman, ISO, and the
  LinkedIn-post that informed our thinking are NOT cited in the
  draft. The voice is Spaarke; the argument is ours.
- **Do not name internal product code paths** in §5 (Spaarke
  surfaces). Use the conceptual surface names plus real product
  capabilities. No SprkChat, SpaarkeAi, three-pane shell, or
  BroadcastChannel references.
