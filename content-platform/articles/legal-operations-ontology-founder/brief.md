# Topic

The founder post for article 3 of the Legal Operations Intelligence series,
the day after the company-page post at
`legal-operations-ontology-syndication`.

# Angle / Point of view

The company post argues that the ontology is the department's intellectual
capital. This post makes the same case from the other end, through the thing
a founder has watched happen: the definitions were never decided. They
accumulated. A department discovers its definition of "matter" only when two
systems disagree in a report, and by then the term is load bearing in
several places at once.

The point that follows is that this is not fixed by buying anything. It is
fixed by giving one person the job of owning the definitions.

# Why now

The article published this week, and the company-page post ran two days
earlier on the asymmetry between the model layer and the definitions layer.
This post supplies the practitioner's view of how the problem arises, which
the company page cannot credibly do.
