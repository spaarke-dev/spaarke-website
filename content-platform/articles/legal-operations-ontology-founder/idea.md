# Idea: founder post for the ontology article

Raw notes, 2026-09-23. Companion to
`legal-operations-ontology-syndication`, which is the company-page post.

Per the campaign, the founder post follows two days after the company post,
in the first person, and takes a different angle so the two do not read as
the same post twice.

The company post argues the asymmetry between the model layer and the
definitions layer. The founder post should do what a founder post can do and
a company post cannot: say what I have seen.

The observation worth making is that nobody decides what "matter" means. It
gets set, once, by whoever configured the first system that needed the field,
and then every later system inherits or contradicts it. By the time a
department notices, the definition is load bearing in four places and
changing it means reconciling four systems.

That is not a technology problem and it does not get fixed by buying
anything. It gets fixed by one person being given the job.
