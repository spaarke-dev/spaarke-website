I've spent my career working with some of the brightest and most successful legal teams in the world — and seeing firsthand the operational challenges they face every day.

Spaarke grew out of a conviction that several major shifts are now converging at the same time: AI is forcing a fundamental rethink of legal operations and the practice of law itself; the way enterprise software is built and delivered has changed dramatically; and platform ecosystems — particularly Microsoft — now create a uniquely powerful foundation for how legal technology can be deployed, governed, and scaled.

The first wave of legal AI focused on individual attorney tasks. Useful tools — but I increasingly believed the larger opportunity was operational: applying AI to how legal work itself runs.

That thinking became Spaarke.

Today we're publishing "Welcome to Spaarke," the foundational article behind the category we believe is emerging: Legal Operations Intelligence.

We believe legal operations is entering its intelligence era. Spaarke is being built for that transition.
