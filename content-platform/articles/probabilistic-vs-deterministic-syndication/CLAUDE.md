## 6. Workflow

Six gates (see `tasks.md` for the done-when checklist per gate):

1. **Outline** — plan.md is generated; **awaiting human sign-off
   before draft starts**
2. **Draft** — write to `draft.md` in this directory (note: `.md` not
   `.mdx` — LinkedIn posts are markdown, not MDX)
3. **Revise** — apply reviewer feedback in same file
4. **Polish** — hook reads cold, hashtags ≤3, byline correct, avoid-this
   sweep, no copy-paste from the source article opening
5. **Image** — existing LinkedIn header at
   `public/articles/probabilistic-vs-deterministic/linkedin-1920x1080.png`
   (no new generation needed)
6. **Ship** — move to `published/linkedin-posts/probabilistic-vs-deterministic-syndication.md`,
   post manually to LinkedIn (Spaarke company page), update calendar +
   campaign + Project + Issue

## 7. What you don't do here

- Do not auto-post to LinkedIn. Posting is human-driven, manual.
- Do not exceed 2 hashtags. The brief locks `#LegalOps #LegalTech`.
- Do not rewrite the hook. The brief locks it verbatim. If you find a
  stronger candidate, escalate via the Unresolved list in the brief —
  do not silently swap.
- Do not reuse the calculator/advisor analogy from the source article's
  opening as the syndication's hook or body. That analogy is the
  article's hook; surfacing it here burns the click-earner.
- Do not write under a named byline (e.g., `ralph-schroeder`). The byline
  is `spaarke` (organizational). A founder-voice version is a separate
  follow-up piece, decided post-publish based on metrics.
- Do not exceed 250 words in the body. If the draft runs over, drop
  Beat 5 (the "what tools should make visible" payoff) first.
