## Spine

> Generative AI is probabilistic, and that property — not a defect — determines where AI belongs in legal work and where it doesn't. The take has specific consequences: deterministic systems still own rules and math; probabilistic systems earn judgement and surfacing; the strongest legal patterns are hybrid; and agent systems compound probabilistic uncertainty unless they're bounded. The post is a **teaser, not a summary** — it lands the take, gestures at one or two consequences, and earns the click back to the full primer. The reader who sees the post should feel they've encountered a sharp argument, not a recap of one.
