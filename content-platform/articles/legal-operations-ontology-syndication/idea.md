# Idea: LinkedIn syndication for the ontology article

Raw notes from the writer, 2026-09-23.

## What this is

Promote article 3 of the Legal Operations Intelligence series, The Legal
Operations Intelligence Ontology, now that all five articles are live at
https://www.spaarke.com/why-spaarke/legal-operations-ontology

The whole series published together, dated June through September. The post
should say this is part of a five-part series, because the series is the
proof that we have thought this through rather than written one piece.

## Why this article and not another

It is the one that defines the foundation of what we build. The other four
describe the mandate, the platform, the deterministic side and the memory
layer; this one names the thing underneath all of them.

## The angle

The article's own thesis: the ontology is the department's intellectual
capital. It is proprietary, it takes years to build, no vendor can build it
for you, and it outlasts whichever AI model you happen to be using this
year. A department that invests only in the model layer is renting.

The uncomfortable corollary for the reader: the definitions of "matter",
"budget" and "obligation" are being set today, tool by tool, by whoever
configured each system. That is a decision nobody made on purpose.

## Format

Per the campaign, push week 3 carries two posts:

- Company page, on publish day.
- Founder post from Ralph, two days later, in the first person.

Article syndication teaser, 200 to 250 words, linking to the article. The
teaser is its own hook and does not summarise the article.

## Sequencing

This is the first promotion of the series. The next slot, October, is the
standalone State of Legal Operations Fall 2026 piece.
