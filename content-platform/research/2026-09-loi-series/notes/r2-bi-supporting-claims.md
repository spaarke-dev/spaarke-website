# R2 fact-check and extension: "A Business Intelligence Approach to Legal Operations"

Checked 2026-09-21 for the article whose display date is now 2026-09-01. Sources dated on or before 2026-09-01 are usable.

## Method and access notes

- WebSearch was available for this pass (unlike the four researcher tracks, which ran with no search budget). Every claim below was checked by fetching the cited page. Where a page is a PDF, the text was extracted locally with pypdf and read directly. Where the WebFetch summarizer truncated a quote, the raw HTML was pulled with curl and the sentence copied from the page text.
- Blocked or unreachable: gartner.com (HTTP 403 on the newsroom release and the glossary), acc.com HTML pages (bot wall, "Checking connection"; static PDFs under acc.com/sites/default/files load), legalops.com through WebFetch (403) but HTTP 200 through curl with browser headers. The LegalOps.com practice-area diagram exists only as an image; it was downloaded, converted to PNG, and inspected visually.
- The CLOC Global Institute program is rendered client side. The public Sessionboard agenda feed behind the page (embed id bf699bda-0971-49fd-a6eb-b9e274de521d, dataType=agenda) returned 351 agenda items and was parsed for the sessions in row T12.
- Every quotation below was seen verbatim on the source unless marked "summarizer only".
