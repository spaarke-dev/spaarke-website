- stagingmain.legalops.com/about-us: Brenton "Co-Founder & Co-CEO ... former CEO of CLOC"; Franke "Co-Founder/Co-CEO and GC"; page says they founded CLOC in 2016, Franke helped create "CLOC's 12 Core Competencies"; Brenton co-authored the handbook "Running Legal Like a Business".
- ABA Journal (Ari Kaplan, 2023-07-24) describes both as CLOC co-founders and founders of LegalOps.com with "an inaugural conference in October". It does NOT mention the book.
- LawVision (Susan Raridon Lambreth, 2023-10-30, https://lawvision.com/the-inaugural-rllb/): conference created by CLOC founders Brenton and Franke via LegalOps.com, partnering with Women's General Counsel Network and LawVision.
- Book: PLI Press, Dec 2021, Connie Brenton and Susan Raridon Lambreth (seen via search-result snippets for pli.edu / Amazon, not the PLI page itself).
- legaloperators.com/about: "Started in 2019 ... local meet up in a neighborhood bar"; promotes "Summit by the Sea 2025"; no mention of RLLB/Brenton. Colin McCarthy as CEO/founder seen only in search snippets (Geek in Review, CCBJ, RocketReach) — the Legal Operators pages I fetched do not name him.

## 4. Four GC paradigms — CONFIRMED
- ABA Journal, Ari Kaplan, "Legal ops co-founders discuss 'the value of running legal like a business'", July 24, 2023. Franke: "(1) ... risk manager, and there was no legal operations support; (2) ... business partner ...; (3) GCs are now strategic and trusted advisers; and (4) the ultimate paradigm is the general counsel who is helping to create a competitive advantage for the business." Then: "Those four paradigms result in four distinct legal operations roles—from tactical, cost-cutting and efficiency-focused to supporting and driving strategy."
- Role scope: "(1) finding the right tools to help deliver services; (2) the delivery of those services; and (3) the core elements of the business—from finance and communication to technology and organizational design."
