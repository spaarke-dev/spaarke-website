# AI Across the Lifecycle

## Theme

July moves from architecture (June) to AI specifics. The argument:
legal AI isn't one feature — it's a layer that operates across the
lifecycle of a matter, on both sides of the engagement, and the
architecture decisions made early determine whether AI is useful
or just present. The July arc is for the GC who has to make AI
procurement calls and the Director of Legal Ops who has to
operationalize them.

## Why now

Mid-year is when 2026 AI procurement decisions get made for the
back half of the year. The questions in the field have matured —
"do you have AI?" has been replaced by "where does our data go,"
"who controls the grounding," and "how does this work with outside
counsel." This campaign answers all three.

## Narrative arc

1. **What attorneys need to know about AI** — the four
   architectural decisions a legal team makes whether it knows
   it or not (existing, recently revised)
2. **Operational intelligence vs productivity AI** *(new Jul 7)* —
   why the two AI categories are different and why the
   distinction matters
3. **AI without giving away the keys** — the data-control case for
   keeping AI grounding inside the customer's tenant
4. **Embedded AI vs bolted-on AI** *(new Jul 14, architecture
   byline)* — the integration argument: where AI lives in the
   workflow determines whether anyone uses it
5. **The AI readiness gap** — what a department needs in place
   structurally before AI delivers value
6. **AI across the engagement** *(promote from Q3 idea bucket)* —
   the standalone treatment of the engagement-boundary theme
   currently woven through three articles
