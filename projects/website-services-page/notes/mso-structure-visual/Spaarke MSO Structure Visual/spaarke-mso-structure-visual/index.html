How the structure works

Your firm keeps the practice. Spaarke runs the operations.

The law firm stays 100% lawyer-owned. Its back office moves into a dedicated MSO that Spaarke owns and runs on its platform, and Spaarke pays for the move. The firm puts in no cash, lowers its operating cost and receives equity in Spaarke.

Ownership Money Services Firm direction Value to the firm

The MSO does not practise law, does not share in legal fees and does not direct legal judgment. Its fee is fixed and set by an independent fair-market-value study, never tied to legal fees.
