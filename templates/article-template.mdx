## Opening hook

Start with the concrete claim, scenario, or stat that makes the article
feel necessary. Not "in today's fast-paced world..." — give the reader a
reason to keep reading by sentence two.

## Section heading

Each `## ` becomes an entry in the right-side **On this page** TOC if the
article has 3 or more H2/H3 headings. Use sentence case; aim for 4–8 words
per heading. Headings are also linkable (each gets an auto-generated `id`).

### Sub-section

`### ` headings appear indented under their H2 in the TOC. Use sparingly —
deep nesting hurts skimming.

## How to add an inline image

Place the file in the same folder as the hero:

    public/articles/your-slug/diagram-1.png

Reference it in MDX:

```markdown
![Short, descriptive alt text](/articles/your-slug/diagram-1.png)
```

If the image is purely decorative, use empty alt text: `![](...)`. If it
conveys information, describe the information in the alt text — not just
the visual ("Bar chart of legal spend by department, FY2025").

## Closing

End with the implication — what the reader should do, watch for, or
take away. Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences) read better than long ones.
